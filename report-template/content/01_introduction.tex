\section{Introduction}

Modern engineering reports frequently combine analytical derivations, numerical simulation,
source code, experimental observations, and design decisions. A reusable template should make
those elements consistent without forcing the author to rewrite page styles, code-listing rules,
or mathematical macros for every project. The organization used here follows the same modular
principle as a well-structured engineering system: metadata, configuration, content, and evidence
are separated into maintainable components.

The fictitious example models a uniform linear array and an edge-processing node. Array
processing provides a compact demonstration because it connects physical geometry, complex
signals, covariance matrices, optimization, and implementation constraints. Standard antenna
and array concepts may be developed from established references such as
\textcite{stutzman2012} and \textcite{vanTrees2002}.

\begin{objective}
Demonstrate a complete technical-report workflow while keeping all project-specific content
replaceable through the files listed in \path{content/00_report_body.tex}.
\end{objective}
